THIS ISN’T MALDIVES… IT’S ABRAKA, NIGERIA 

Where else in Nigeria can you find clear BLUE waters, natural white sand, and a river that looks like it was poured straight out of the sky?

Welcome to River Ethiope – Delta State’s best-kept secret. 🌊✨

Born from the roots of an ancient silk-cotton tree in Umuaja, this spring-fed river flows gently through Abraka, creating scenes that feel almost unreal.

📍 Location: Abraka, Delta State
💧 Source: Umuaja, Delta State
🏝️ Vibe: White sand. Blue water. Wild peace.

Whether you're floating in silence, capturing drone shots, or just soaking your feet by the shore – River Ethiope isn’t just beautiful... it’s magical.

🎥: Shot at McCarthy Beach Resort
